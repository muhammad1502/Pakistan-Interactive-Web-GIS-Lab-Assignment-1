This interactive atlas was created for Lab Assignment 1 in Web GIS Application. It brings two spatial datasets together in a responsive, accessible interface: major Pakistani cities and weather stations.

Good to know
Weather values are a static educational dataset prepared to demonstrate GIS interaction—not live observations.
